How to reduce refused deliveries in cash on delivery

A refused parcel costs you the ad, the shipping and often the return. Most refusals are decided long before the courier knocks, which means most of them can be prevented.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz Founder & CEO, CTRL Solutions 21 November 20263 min read

In cash-on-delivery commerce, the most painful order is the one that almost happened. You paid for the ad, you packed the parcel, you paid the courier, and at the door the buyer says no. Sometimes you also pay to bring it back.

Refusals never go to zero. But in the stores I've worked with, most of them trace back to a handful of causes, and each cause has a fix.

Why buyers refuse at the door

  • They forgot they ordered. An impulse order at midnight, delivered four days later, can feel like a surprise.
  • The price surprised them. Shipping fees they didn't notice, or a total that differs from what they expected.
  • The product isn't what they imagined. Photos that oversell, sizes that weren't clear, a description that skipped the important part.
  • Delivery took too long. In the meantime they bought elsewhere, or simply cooled down.
  • Nobody confirmed the order. Wrong numbers, incomplete addresses and fake orders go out the door unchecked.
  • They don't have the cash right then. Especially when the courier arrives at an inconvenient time.

Fixes before the order

The cheapest refusal to prevent is the one you never create.

  • Show the real product. Real photos and videos, close-ups, the size in a hand. Overselling in the ad creates refusals at the door.
  • Say the total early. Product price, delivery fee and total, visible before the order button. No surprises.
  • Promise delivery times you can keep. "Delivery in 2 to 4 days" is only helpful if it's true.

I go deeper into trust in trust signals for COD stores.

Fixes at the order form

  • Keep the form short, but make sure the phone number and address are usable.
  • Validate the phone number gently so typos don't become lost orders.
  • Repeat the order summary on the thank-you page, including the total and what happens next.

More in the COD order form article.

Fixes after the order

  • Confirm within minutes. A quick WhatsApp message or call filters out fake orders and reminds real buyers why they ordered. I explain the flow in confirming COD orders on WhatsApp.
  • Don't ship unconfirmed orders. It feels like losing a sale. It's usually saving money.
  • Send a message the day before delivery. A short "your order arrives tomorrow" gives buyers time to prepare the cash and be available.
  • Keep track of repeat refusers. If the same number refuses again and again, ask for confirmation by call before shipping, or ask for a partial payment upfront.

Measure it every week

Track your delivery rate: delivered orders out of shipped orders. Break it down by product, by city and by campaign if you can. A campaign that brings cheap orders with a low delivery rate is not cheap. Sometimes the "best" ad in Ads Manager is the worst one in the bank account.
