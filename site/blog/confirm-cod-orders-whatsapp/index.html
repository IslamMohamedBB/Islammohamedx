Confirming COD orders on WhatsApp: the follow-up that protects your delivery rate

In cash-on-delivery commerce, the order form is only half the sale. The other half happens in the next few hours, when someone confirms that the buyer still wants it.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz Founder & CEO, CTRL Solutions 21 November 20263 min read

Every COD store owner knows this feeling: the dashboard shows a good day of orders, and a week later the courier report tells a different story. Some numbers didn't answer, some buyers changed their minds, some addresses were incomplete, and some parcels came back.

You can't remove all of that, but a fast, friendly confirmation removes a lot of it. Here's how I set it up for the stores we run.

Why confirmation matters so much

A COD order costs you money before it earns any. You paid for the ad that brought it, you'll pay to ship it, and if it's refused you may pay to bring it back. Confirmation is the cheapest moment to find out whether the order is real.

It also helps the buyer. They ordered in a few seconds, often late at night, from an ad. A message that repeats what they ordered, the total and when it will arrive turns an impulse into a decision they've agreed to twice.

Speed is everything

The best time to confirm is within minutes of the order, while the buyer still remembers the ad and the product. The longer you wait, the more likely they are to forget, to compare prices elsewhere, or to stop answering unknown numbers.

That's why every new order in our stores reaches the team instantly. I explain the setup in why every order should ping your phone.

What the message should say

Keep it short, warm and specific. A good confirmation message includes:

  • A greeting with the buyer's name.
  • The product, variant and quantity they ordered.
  • The total, including delivery, so there's no surprise at the door.
  • The delivery address as they wrote it, so they can correct it.
  • The expected delivery window.
  • One simple question: "Shall we confirm and ship it?"

Write it in the language your buyers speak. For Egyptian buyers, a natural Egyptian Arabic message feels like a person, not a system.

When they don't reply

Not everyone answers WhatsApp quickly. My usual sequence is a WhatsApp message first, then a phone call a few hours later if there's no reply, then one last message the next day. If there's still nothing, the order is marked unconfirmed and doesn't ship. Shipping an unconfirmed order is often the most expensive decision in the whole funnel.

Small upsells, carefully

Confirmation is also a natural moment to offer something useful: a second size, a matching product, a bundle that saves delivery. Done gently, it raises the order value. Done aggressively, it makes people cancel. One offer, once, is enough. I wrote more about this in raising average order value without discounts.

What to track

Every week I look at three simple numbers for a COD store:

  1. Confirmation rate: confirmed orders out of all orders.
  2. Delivery rate: delivered orders out of shipped orders.
  3. Time to confirm: how long orders wait before the first message.

If the confirmation rate drops, something changed in the ads, the offer or the order form. If the delivery rate drops, look at the courier, the delivery time and the confirmation script. I go deeper into refusals in reducing refused deliveries.

The ad gets the order. Confirmation keeps it. If your store loses too many orders between the form and the door, message me on WhatsApp and tell me how you confirm today.
