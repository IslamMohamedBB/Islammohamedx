What an ERP for a perfume business must do

Ads bring the customers. What happens behind the counter decides whether the business keeps the money. Here's what I learned building ERP and point-of-sale systems for perfume businesses.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz Founder & CEO, CTRL Solutions 21 November 20263 min read

Marketing gets the attention, but I've seen more perfume businesses lose money behind the counter than in Ads Manager. Stock that doesn't match the shelves, prices that differ between branches, cash that nobody can reconcile at the end of the day, and owners who only find out what happened a week later.

That's why CTRL builds systems, not only campaigns. Two examples from our work: DD STORE, a multi-branch ERP and point-of-sale system for a perfume company, and a shop POS for another perfume business. Here's what a perfume business actually needs from a system like this.

1. One stock, every branch

The first job is a single source of truth for stock. Every branch sells from its own shelf, but the owner needs to see all of it in one place, move stock between branches, and know what's running low before customers find out. Without that, the best-selling perfume is always "out of stock" in the branch where people want it.

2. Retail and wholesale in the same system

Many perfume businesses sell both to walk-in customers and to merchants who buy in bulk. Those are different prices, different invoices and often different payment terms. A good system handles both without someone keeping a second notebook. For merchants who pay later, it should track what each one owes, which is why one of our systems records merchant debts.

3. Barcodes that never change

Perfume shops carry hundreds of similar-looking items. Permanent barcodes for every product make selling fast and stock counts accurate. Typing names at the counter is where mistakes start.

4. Manufacturing, when the business makes its own

Some perfume businesses fill and produce their own products. That means tracking raw materials, bottles and finished items, and knowing what a finished product really costs. DD STORE includes manufacturing for exactly this reason.

5. Cashier permissions

Not everyone at the counter should be able to change prices, give discounts or delete invoices. Clear roles protect the business and the staff. A cashier sells, a manager approves, an owner sees everything.

6. Receipts and alerts people actually read

Paper receipts get lost. One of the systems we built sends invoices on WhatsApp, which customers keep and owners can trace. And because owners can't stand in every branch, our systems send live Telegram alerts for sales and a daily report at the end of the day. I wrote about that in running a business from your phone.

7. Attendance

With several branches and shifts, attendance becomes a real problem. DD STORE records attendance with a photo, so the owner knows who opened each branch and when.

Why custom, not off the shelf

There are many ready-made POS systems. For some shops they're enough. Perfume businesses with branches, wholesale customers and their own production usually end up bending their work around software that wasn't built for them. A system built around how the business actually works is faster for staff and clearer for the owner. I explain when custom is worth it in when you need a custom system.

If your shop runs on notebooks, spreadsheets and phone calls between branches, tell me how you work today on WhatsApp. That's usually where the first version of the system starts.
