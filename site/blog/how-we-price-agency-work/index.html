How we price agency work: packages built around goals

"How much?" is the first question almost every brand asks us. The honest answer is a short conversation, because the price depends on what the brand needs to achieve. Here's how we think about it.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz Founder & CEO, CTRL Solutions 21 November 20263 min read

On our website, the pricing answer is deliberately simple: we work with monthly packages built around your goal, based on the number of videos, the channels and the ad budget. Send us your brand and what you need, and we'll come back with a proposal.

I know that can feel evasive. So here's what actually goes into the price, and why we don't publish a single number.

Why one price doesn't fit

Two brands can both ask for "social media and ads" and need completely different things. One needs eight reels a month, page management and ads on one platform. Another needs a store rebuilt, a daily content rhythm, creator videos and ads across several products. Publishing one package price would either overcharge the first or underserve the second.

What drives the price

  • Content volume: how many videos, posts and stories each month, and whether they need shooting, editing or creators.
  • Channels: Facebook and Instagram only, or TikTok too, and how many pages or accounts.
  • Ads scope: how many products and campaigns, how much testing, and the size and complexity of the ad budget we manage.
  • Community and inbox: whether we manage replies and comments, and for how many hours.
  • Builds: stores, websites, dashboards and systems are usually priced as projects, separate from the monthly work.

What's separate from our fee

The ad budget itself. Money spent on Meta or TikTok ads goes to the platform. Our fee is for planning, creating, running and optimizing. Keeping them separate makes it clear what you pay for the work and what you pay for the reach.

What every ads package includes

Whatever the size, our ads work always stands on the same foundations: the Pixel and Conversions API set up correctly, clear goals agreed at the start, and clear weekly reporting on return on ad spend, cost per order and real orders, not likes. I describe that report in what a weekly report should tell you.

How to compare proposals

When you compare agencies, compare deliverables, not package names: how many videos, which channels, who shoots, how many new ads are tested each week, and what gets reported and how often. A cheaper package that produces half the content isn't really cheaper.

What we don't promise

We don't promise a specific return on ad spend before we've seen your numbers, your product and your funnel. Anyone who promises 10X in the first meeting is selling the meeting. We publish our real results, including a blended 3.0X across our ad accounts, so brands can see what's realistic.

How the conversation usually goes

  1. You tell us the brand, what you sell and what you want to achieve.
  2. We look at what exists: pages, store, ads, numbers.
  3. We suggest the shortcuts that fit, from ads to content, video, stores and creators, and a monthly scope.
  4. You get a proposal with clear deliverables, so you can compare it to what you're paying today.
