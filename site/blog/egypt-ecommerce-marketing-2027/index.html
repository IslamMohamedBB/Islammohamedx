What I expect from e-commerce marketing in Egypt in 2027

Predictions are cheap. These are expectations built on what our ad accounts and brand pages showed this year. Here's what I expect from 2027, and what I'd prepare for now.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz Founder & CEO, CTRL Solutions 21 November 20263 min read

I don't trust predictions that come without numbers, so here are mine with the numbers behind them. They're expectations, not promises, and I'll revisit them next year.

1. Short video carries even more

The 18 brand pages we manage reached more than 64.3 million views on Facebook in 2026 so far. I expect brands that can produce good short videos every week to keep pulling ahead of brands that post occasionally. I break the views down in 64.3M views across 18 pages.

2. Conversations stay central

Our campaigns have started more than 101,000 conversations. For many Egyptian brands, the inbox is still where a large part of the selling happens. I expect fast replies, automation for the first answer and good confirmation calls to matter as much as the ads themselves. More in reply speed is a sales metric.

3. Facebook stays big, and Instagram matters more in some categories

In our accounts, Facebook still carried most purchases for a laptop store and an online store, while a perfume brand's purchases split almost evenly between Facebook and Instagram. I expect that split to keep depending on the category and the audience, not on fashion. The data is in Facebook vs Instagram in Egypt.

4. TikTok gets real budgets, and real tests

According to Egypt's Information and Decision Support Center, TikTok's adult audience in Egypt is now close to Facebook's in size. I expect more brands to test it seriously in 2027. The ones that win will use creator-style content and judge results by delivered orders. I explain how in TikTok or Meta.

5. More brands own their store and their systems

In 2026 we moved a store off Shopify to our own template and built order forms, dashboards and alert systems for the stores we run. I expect more brands to want control over their checkout, their data and their costs.

6. Offers beat discounts

In the stores we run, we've moved toward clear bundles, like any three for one price, instead of scattered percentage discounts. I expect simple, generous-feeling offers to keep winning, which I explain in offers that convert.

7. Delivered orders become the number that matters

I expect more brand owners to ask how many orders were actually delivered and paid, not just how many Ads Manager reports. That question should shape how agencies are judged. More in CPA, ROAS and delivered orders.

8. AI speeds up production, people keep the voice

AI tools already help us produce visuals and drafts faster. But the voice that sells to Egyptian buyers is still a real Egyptian voice. I expect the winners to use AI for speed and keep people for trust.

9. The year starts early

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February. That means the year's first big season arrives just weeks after New Year. Brands that plan in December will be ready. I've laid out the dates in seasonal campaigns in Egypt.

What I'd do now

  • Lock in a weekly video rhythm.
  • Fix the inbox: speed, saved replies and the first automatic answer.
  • Track delivered orders per campaign.
  • Plan Ramadan before the end of December.

If you want to plan 2027 for your brand, message me on WhatsApp with what you sell and what worked this year.
