Attention is cheap. Action is everything.

Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz, founder and CEO of CTRL Solutions, on the work he loves, the market he builds for, and why every number matters.

Islam Mohamed AbdelazizFounder & CEO, CTRL Solutions14 November 2026
@islammohamedx @ctrlsolutions.eg Start a project

I have never been interested in marketing that only looks good.

A beautiful reel that doesn't move a single order is a missed opportunity. A page with thousands of likes and an empty inbox is a problem dressed up as success. That belief is the whole reason CTRL Solutions exists, and it sits in our bio for anyone to hold us to: we turn attention into action.

Where it started

I came into this field from the creative side. I was a content creator first, then a social media specialist, then a media buyer, and each step taught me something the one before it couldn't. Content taught me how people feel. Social media taught me how they behave. Media buying taught me what all of it is worth.

The lesson I took from all three is simple. Creativity without numbers is guessing, and numbers without creativity are noise. The brands that win need both in the same room, every day. CTRL is that room.

Why “CTRL”

Most brand owners I meet don't need another vendor. They need control. Their ads spend without explaining themselves, their page posts without growing, their store gets visits but not orders. Every one of those is a key that's stuck.

So we built the agency around five shortcuts: Ctrl+A for ads, Ctrl+C for content, Ctrl+V for video, Ctrl+S for stores and Ctrl+T for talents. Some brands need one. Most need all five working together, and that is where real growth happens.

The market I build for

I build for the Egyptian buyer, and I love this market for how demanding it is. Cash on delivery is the norm, people compare prices in seconds, and trust is never given for free. You can't fake your way through that. Every detail counts: the first three seconds of a video, the photo on a product page, the number of fields in an order form, how fast someone replies on WhatsApp after the order.

That is why we don't talk in likes. We talk in ROAS, CPA, average order value and conversion rate, because those are the numbers that decide whether a business survives.

We don't run ads. We build revenue systems.Islam Mohamed Abdelaziz

Proof, not promises

Our results come with their sources. When we took over the DD Perfumes Store page, it gained 25,400 new followers in 28 days, a 770% increase, with 4.1 million views, 93% of them organic, and a record day of 830,000 views. Those numbers come straight from Meta Business Suite.

+770%new followers in 28 days
4.1Mviews, 93% organic
16XROAS in 30 days

On the performance side, we took an online store that was burning budget with nothing to show for it, rebuilt its funnel and added smart retargeting. In 30 days it made 1,046,225 EGP in sales at a 16X return on ad spend. No luck and no hype, just strategy, testing and execution.

When the tool doesn't exist, we build it

Somewhere along the way I realized that a lot of what slows brands down isn't marketing at all. It's the tools around it. So we started building our own.

Today CTRL designs and launches stores on Shopify and Wuilt, builds fully custom websites and admin dashboards, and runs ERP and point-of-sale systems for multi-branch businesses. We built CTRL Bio for one link in bio, CTRL Reply to answer every comment with a private message, and a cash-on-delivery order form for Shopify stores. And because content made by real people sells, we built UGC ARABIA, a platform that connects brands with UGC creators across the Arab world.

What keeps me going

People ask me where the passion comes from. Honestly, it comes from the small moments: a new store receiving its first order, a client sending me a screenshot of their best day ever, a creator getting paid for work they're proud of. Those moments never get old.

I love this field because it never stands still. The algorithm changes, the audience changes, the tools change, and every change is a new chance for the brand that moves fast. I wake up wanting to find that chance before anyone else does.

What's next

We're growing CTRL across Egypt and the Arab world, we already work with brands in Jordan, and we're building UGC ARABIA into the home of Arab UGC creators. The mission stays the same: put the controls back in the hands of the people building brands.

If you have a brand and you're ready to take control, send us a message. Tell us your brand and what you need. We'll take it from there.

Islam Mohamed AbdelazizFounder & CEO, CTRL Solutions
