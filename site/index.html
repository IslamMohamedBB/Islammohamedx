Questions brands ask us

What does CTRL Solutions do?

CTRL Solutions is a digital growth agency in Egypt. We run Meta and TikTok ads, manage content and social media, shoot and edit videos and reels, design and launch online stores and websites, and manage talents and UGC creators.

Do you work with brands outside Egypt?

Yes. Most of our clients are in Egypt, and we also work with brands across the Arab world, such as Zakharef Al Nasr in Jordan.

Which platforms do you advertise on?

Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp) and TikTok. We set up the Pixel and Conversions API so every campaign is measured on real orders.

Can you build our online store?

Yes. We build on Shopify, on Wuilt, or fully custom, and connect the store to your ads, COD order forms and order notifications. A2Z Perfumes, Lavie Perfumes, Zan2t Satat and PRIZERO are some of the stores we launched.

How much does it cost?

We work with monthly packages built around your goal: the number of videos, the channels and the ad budget. Send us your brand and what you need, and we will reply with a proposal.

How fast will we see results?

It depends on your product, offer and budget. Ad tests start in the first week and you see the numbers as they come in. For DD Perfumes Store, the page hit its best numbers ever within 28 days.

Do you work with UGC creators and influencers?

Yes. Our platform UGC ARABIA connects brands with UGC creators across the Arab world, and our team handles the brief, usage rights and delivery.

How do we start?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with your brand name and what you need. We reply as fast as we can and set a short call to understand your goal.
